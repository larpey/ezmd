# The Lighthouse Keeper {#doc}

<!-- page 1 -->

## 1 Chapter One: The Lamp {#sec-1}

Every evening at dusk, *Elin* climbed the **one hundred and twelve** steps of the tower to light the lamp.[^1] The wick had to be trimmed first.

![Hand-drawn map of the harbor](images/harbor-map.png)
Figure 1: The harbor as Elin drew it.
<!-- image: images/harbor-map.png page=1 -->

She kept a logbook of every ship that passed.

<!-- page 2 -->

By midnight the beam swept the water every `12` seconds.

[^1]: The original tower had only ninety steps; the rest were added in 1871.

## 2 Chapter Two: The Harbor {#sec-2}

The harbor woke before the keeper slept. Three things arrived each morning:

- the fishing fleet,
- the mail boat, and
    1. its letters
    2. its parcels
- the gulls.

## 3 Chapter Three: The Storm {#sec-3}

<!-- page 4 -->

The storm came from the west on the ninth night.[^2]

Elin wrote the signal code on the wall:

```text
LONG SHORT LONG
SHORT SHORT
```

When morning came, the lamp was still burning.

[^2]: Storms from the west were recorded in the logbook with a red cross.
