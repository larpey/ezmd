# Notes from the Salt Flats {#doc}

## 1 Evaporation {#sec-1}

A shallow pan loses about four millimetres of water a day in July.[^1] The salt that remains is raked into rows.

The evaporation rate is $E = k (e_s - e_a)$ where *k* depends on wind.

[^1]: Measured at the north pans in 2023.

## 2 Glossary {#sec-2}

**Brine**

Water saturated with salt.

**Fleur de sel**

The thin crust skimmed from the surface.
