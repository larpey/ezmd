# Port safety handbook {#doc}

<!-- page 1 -->

This handbook applies to every person working on the quay.

## 1 Personal protective equipment {#sec-1}

Hard hats and high-visibility vests are required at all times.

## 2 Night work {#sec-2}

Night shifts need a second person within sight at all times.
