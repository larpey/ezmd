# Tidal Patterns in a Small Harbor {#doc}

<!-- page 1 -->

Tides in the inner harbor follow a semidiurnal pattern with two highs and two lows each lunar day. The range at the north wall averages two point one meters, and spring tides add roughly forty centimeters to that figure. Gauges were read every ten minutes for a full year.

Wind set-up matters more than expected. A steady southerly wind above twenty knots raised the observed level by up to thirty centimeters for several hours. Pilots now consult the wind forecast together with the tide table before scheduling deep-draft arrivals.

Both columns together describe one year of observations.
