Field Notes on Cold Storage
===========================

The depot keeps three walk-in freezers at minus eighteen degrees. Each is
checked twice per shift, and readings go into the paper log by the door.

Door Seals
----------

Seals on freezer two were replaced in March. The technician noted frost
build-up near the hinge side; the café staff were asked to keep the door shut.

Temperatures above minus fifteen for more than ten minutes trigger an alarm —
it rings at the front desk and on the manager's phone. Résumé of the last
incident: alarm at 02:14, door found ajar, closed at 02:19, no stock lost.

Emoji and symbols survive too: ✓ checked, € budget, 日本語 label.
