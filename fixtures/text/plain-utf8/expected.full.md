# Field Notes on Cold Storage {#doc}

The depot keeps three walk-in freezers at minus eighteen degrees. Each is checked twice per shift, and readings go into the paper log by the door.

## 1 Door Seals {#sec-1}

Seals on freezer two were replaced in March. The technician noted frost build-up near the hinge side; the café staff were asked to keep the door shut.

Temperatures above minus fifteen for more than ten minutes trigger an alarm — it rings at the front desk and on the manager's phone. Résumé of the last incident: alarm at 02:14, door found ajar, closed at 02:19, no stock lost.
