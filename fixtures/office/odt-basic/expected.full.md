# Garden Pond Notes {#doc}

A small pond attracts **frogs** within a season[^1]. See the [wildlife guide](https://example.org/wildlife-ponds).

[^1]: Usually common frogs in the first spring.

## 1 Building steps {#sec-1}

1. Dig a shallow shelf
2. Line the hole
3. Fill with rainwater

## 2 Plants to add {#sec-2}

- Oxygenators
    - Hornwort
    - Water starwort
- Marginals

**Table 1**

| Item | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Liner | 45 |
| Plants | 30 |

Top up with rainwater, never tap water.

Use a flexible liner rated for ponds.
