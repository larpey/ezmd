# Depot Procedures {#doc}

*Opening and closing the bus depot*

## 1 Opening {#sec-1}

1. Unlock the gate
2. Switch on the yard lights
    1. Check the north bank
    2. Check the south bank
3. Start the fuel pump

## 2 Closing {#sec-2}

The closing list restarts its numbering at one.

1. Stop the fuel pump
2. Count the buses
3. Lock the gate

### 2.1 Emergencies {#sec-2-1}

These steps continue the safety manual, which ends at step four.

5. Call the duty manager
6. Clear the yard
