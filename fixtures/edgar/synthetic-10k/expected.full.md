## 3 Item 1. Business {#sec-3}

### 3.1 Overview {#sec-3-1}

Harbor Lane operates a fleet of eleven feeder container vessels on short-sea routes between regional ports. Charter terms are typically twelve to thirty-six months.

### 3.2 Fleet {#sec-3-2}

The fleet had an average age of 9.4 years at December 31, 2025. See Item 7 for charter coverage.

## 4 Item 1A. Risk Factors {#sec-4}

### 4.1 Risks related to our industry {#sec-4-1}

Charter rates are volatile and a sustained decline would reduce revenue and cash flow.

## 5 Item 1B. Unresolved Staff Comments {#sec-5}

None.
