# Saltmarsh Rowing Club {#doc}

**Spring Regatta Results**

The spring regatta ran on a falling tide with a light westerly. Twelve crews raced the 1,000 metre course in three heats.

The junior eight won its first final in a decade, finishing two lengths clear of the visitors.

**Table 1: Final, men's eights**

| Place | Crew | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Saltmarsh Junior | 3:02.4 |
| 2 | Estuary RC | 3:08.9 |
| 3 | Harbor Scullers | 3:11.0 |

Thanks to the launch drivers and the umpires. The autumn head race is on 15 October.
