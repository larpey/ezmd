# Fender Care Basics {#doc}

Fenders protect your hull from the dock. Rinse them with fresh water after each trip and check the lines for chafe before they part.

Inflate vinyl fenders to the maker's pressure. An under-inflated fender rolls and slips out from between hull and pile.

Store fenders out of direct sun to keep the vinyl from cracking.
