# Credits

Third-party material in the golden fixture corpus (`fixtures/`). Every fixture whose `[provenance] origin` is
not `self-generated` has an entry here; `tools/check_fixture_provenance.py` (run by the pytest suite) fails when
an entry is missing, incomplete, or disagrees with the fixture's `meta.toml`. Self-generated fixtures are
CC0-1.0 and are produced by the `_generate.py` script in their family directory.

Allowed origins and licenses: public domain (including U.S. Government works under 17 U.S.C. 105), CC0, and
CC-BY. No commercial media and no platform-scraped content.

Entry format: a `### fixtures/<family>/<name>` heading followed by `- Title:`, `- Author:`, `- License:`
(SPDX id or LicenseRef, same as meta.toml), `- URL:` (same as meta.toml `source`), and `- Retrieved:`
(YYYY-MM-DD) lines.
